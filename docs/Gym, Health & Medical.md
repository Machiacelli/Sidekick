# Sidekick Module Catalogue — Gym, Health & Medical

---

🏋️ **Special Gym Ratios**

Where to find it: Settings → Gym → Special Gym Ratios (also toggleable from the toolbar popup) · On by default · Runs on the gym page

Specialist gyms (Balboas, Frontline, the 1.25× single-stat gyms, etc.) require your stats to stay within ratios — drift out of line and you lose access. This module adds a panel on the gym page where you pick your desired specialist build, then shows a live status line under each of your four stats: green when safely inside the ratio, orange when close, red when you're about to lose the gym. Updated automatically as your stats grow, with a configurable "safe distance" warning margin.

---

💪 **Auto Gym Switch**

Where to find it: Settings → Gym → Auto Gym (toggleable from the toolbar popup) · Off by default · Runs on the gym page

Automatically switches you to the best available gym for the stat you're training before you spend energy. It intercepts the gym page's own requests, so training a stat you've been neglecting never wastes energy on an inferior gym.

---

🚫 **Block Training (Gym Blocker)**

Where to find it: Settings → Gym → Block Training (toggleable from the toolbar popup) · Off by default · Runs on the gym page

Puts an overlay over the entire gym, blocking every training click. For when you're saving energy for a chain, holding stats for a faction requirement, or just know you shouldn't train. Toggle it off and the gym is instantly usable again.

---

#️⃣ **Nice Helper (Merit Calculator)**

Where to find it: Settings → Merits → Enable Nice Helper (toggleable from the toolbar popup) · Off by default · Runs on the gym page

Helps plan the "69 and 420" stat merits — you earn them when a battle stat lands exactly on a value containing 69 or 420. Nice Helper scans your four stats, finds the nearest values that contain a nice pattern, and builds a training plan to hit the best one: which stat to train, which target value to aim for, how many sessions it needs, and which gym to use in each phase so you don't overshoot the number. 420 targets are prioritized as the rarer merit.

---

🩸 **Blood Bag Reminder**

Where to find it: Settings → Medical → Blood Bag Reminder (toggleable from the toolbar popup) · Off by default · Runs on all Torn pages (status area)

When your life is high enough (and your medical cooldown far enough along) to fill one or more blood bags, a blood-bag icon appears in your sidebar status area. Click it to jump straight to your items or the faction armory to fill the bags. Configurable: how many bags you aim to fill (1–3 sets the life % and cooldown threshold) and whether the link opens in a new tab.

---

💉 **Smart Medical Button**

Where to find it: Settings → Medical → Enable Smart Medical Button (toggleable from the toolbar popup) · Off by default · Runs on all Torn pages (floater)

A floating button that shows your hospital time, and while hospitalized displays which medical item (Small FAK, First Aid Kit, Morphine, or a blood bag of your blood type) best covers your remaining sentence — optimized by the item's reduce amount per cooldown minute, adjusted by your education/faction medical perks. Click it to jump to your items or faction armory. Draggable; position saved.

---

⚠️ **Rehab Warning**

Where to find it: Settings → Reminders → Rehab Warning (toggleable from the toolbar popup) · Off by default · Runs on all Torn pages

Monitors your drug addiction level and warns with a persistent corner banner when it crosses your threshold — critical for education courses, since too much addiction gets you kicked from your course. Shows your current addiction %, how many Xanax you can still safely take before crossing the line, and optionally warns about company effectiveness penalties if you work a job where addiction hurts.

---

💊 **Xanax Viewer**

Where to find it: Settings → Personal → Xanax Viewer (toggleable from the toolbar popup) · Off by default · Runs on faction and profile pages · Requires your Torn API key

Adds a Xanax column to your faction's member list (with a refresh button per member) and shows each player's total Xanax count directly on their profile (yours too, auto-detected on your own profile). A standard check for factions enforcing Xanax rules or seeing who's burning energy fast.
